Roblox Product Manager

Create and edit game passes, developer products and badges for your Roblox experiences, many at a time.

How signing in works

  1. Click Sign in with Roblox. You go to Roblox's own page (apis.roblox.com). This app never sees your password.
  2. Log in to Roblox if it asks. You need a 13+ account.
  3. Roblox shows what this app wants to do: view and edit game passes and developer products, and create and edit badges. Creating a badge can spend Robux once the game's free badges for the day are used up. This app always asks you before that happens.
  4. Choose the experiences this app may manage. You can choose your own experiences and group experiences your role is allowed to manage.
  5. Confirm. Roblox sends you back here, signed in.
  6. Pick a game, pick a tab, then create or edit.

Need a different game later? After signing in, click Add or remove games. Signing out revokes this app's access.
